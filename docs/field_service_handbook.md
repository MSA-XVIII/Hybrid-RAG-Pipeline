# Field Service Operations Handbook

## 1 Purpose and Audience
This handbook guides the Regional Service Team through day-to-day operations. It is written for
the Field Engineer, the Duty Lead, and the Regional Specialist. It complements the equipment
service manuals and focuses on process rather than hardware.

## 2 Dispatch and Scheduling
Work orders arrive through the central portal. Each morning the Duty Lead reviews the queue and
assigns jobs based on proximity and skill. Same-day jobs take priority over routine visits, and
the portal records the acceptance time for every assignment.

## 3 Working On Site
On arrival the engineer confirms the site contact, reviews safety notices, and photographs the
equipment nameplate before starting any work.

## 3.1 Escalation and On-Call Rotation
If a problem cannot be resolved within two hours, the engineer contacts the Duty Lead, who may
page the Regional Specialist. Outside business hours the paging tree is followed in order until
someone acknowledges. A weekly roster names who carries the pager each night and weekend.

## 3.2 Parts Ordering and Returns
When a replacement part is required, the engineer raises a request in the portal and selects the
nearest depot. Faulty units are sealed, labelled with the original work order, and shipped back
within three business days so the depot can restock.

## 4 Preventive Care
Routine visits keep equipment healthy between faults and reduce emergency callouts.

## 4.1 Preventive Maintenance Schedule
Every installed unit is visited on a fixed cadence: quarterly for high-use sites and twice a
year elsewhere. The visit checklist covers cleaning, wear inspection, firmware currency, and a
short functional test. The portal reminds the Duty Lead one week before each due date.

## 4.2 Firmware and Software Currency
The team keeps software within one release of the approved baseline. Before any update the
engineer captures the current configuration, applies the package during a maintenance window,
and confirms a clean restart before leaving the site.

## 5 Records and Reporting
Every visit produces a signed report in the portal. Monthly, the Regional Specialist reviews
trends, repeat visits, and open work orders, and shares a summary with the Duty Lead.
